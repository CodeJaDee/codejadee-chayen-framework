## Risks

| ID | Risk | Impact | Mitigation | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | Provider plugin formats are young and change quickly. Assumptions A-01..A-06 may be outdated. | Packaging breaks, or rework is needed. | Verify against official docs in Step 02, record source URLs and dates, keep manifests thin (D-005), and add regression tests (Step 05). | OPEN |
| R-02 | Automatic triggering is probabilistic (A-05). Skills may under-trigger or over-trigger. | Misses REQ-02, or breaks REQ-04 (unwanted activation). | Use precise descriptions, positive and negative trigger suites (Step 10), and measured hit rates instead of claims. | OPEN |
| R-03 | 206 capabilities as separate skills would overload metadata context and degrade routing. | Poorer triggering, and every session pays a context cost. | Map capabilities to fewer skills with references (D-006), enforce size budgets in the validator. | OPEN |
| R-04 | Provider feature parity is uneven. A feature may exist in one provider but not another. | REQ-01 and REQ-10 can't be met uniformly. | Keep a per-provider compatibility matrix and record each gap through a decision record instead of hiding it. | OPEN |
| R-05 | Generic best-practice guidance may conflict with a host repository's conventions. | Violates REQ-07. | Detect-before-prescribe rule (D-008), with trigger tests on repositories that use non-default conventions. | OPEN |
| R-06 | Users may read security guidance as protection. | False sense of security, contrary to REQ-09. | Advisory wording (D-003), validator checks for enforcement claims, and pointers to real controls. | OPEN |
| R-07 | Automated testing of Codex and Copilot trigger behaviour may lack official tooling. | REQ-10 evidence may have to be partly manual. | Document a reproducible manual or scripted procedure, record transcripts, and label the results' fidelity honestly. | OPEN |
